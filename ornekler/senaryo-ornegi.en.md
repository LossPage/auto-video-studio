# Why Does the Moon Follow Us?
> tr: Ay Neden Bizi Takip Ediyor?

## Night walk | quiet forest path at night, big full moon above the trees
[visual: Mavi the blue bunny walks on the forest path and looks up at the big moon, Kirpi follows behind]
NARRATOR: One night, Mavi and Kirpi were walking home.
MAVI (surprised): Kirpi, look! The moon is walking with us!
KIRPI (curious): When we stop, does it stop too?
MAVI: Why do you think that is? What do you think?
(pause 2)

## The answer on the hill | grassy hilltop at night, the whole valley and the moon far away
[visual: Mavi and Kirpi sit on the hilltop, the moon is tiny and far beyond the valley]
NARRATOR: The moon is very, very far away. So far that however much we walk, it seems to stay in the same place.
KIRPI (happy): So it isn't following us, it's just really far away!
MAVI (happy): But it still lights our way. Good night, Moon!
